The Founder

Meet Donald.

Donald Williams Jr. is 18, from Metro Detroit, and built A Near Future from the ground up: the magazine, the website, the Builders Board and more than 155 published stories, all while finishing early college.

He studies business administration with an accounting focus through Oakland Early College, where he serves as Vice President of Student Government and made the Dean's List. He was one of about 200 students chosen from more than 2,700 applicants for the Midnight Golf Program, and he has started several businesses of his own.

"I built A Near Future because I wanted to see the people around me reflected in something that took them seriously."

Partnering with ANF means backing a founder who builds the same way the people he covers do.
