25 Detroit Businesses Just Got Picked. Yours Wasn't One.

TechTown Detroit named its 2026 Detroit Elevate cohort, 25 established local companies backed by a $950,000 Gilbert Family Foundation investment. Here is what they won, and how to be on the list next time.

A Near Future Editorial

August 2026

25

Detroit businesses selected for the 2026 Detroit Elevate cohort

TechTown Detroit named the 2026 cohort of Detroit Elevate, and 25 local businesses made the cut. The group spans construction, technology, professional services, food, retail, beauty and the creative arts, a slice of the city's small business base that looks a lot like the city itself. The program is powered by a $950,000 investment from the Gilbert Family Foundation, and it is built to do the one thing capital alone rarely does: move an established business from surviving to scaling.

Detroit Elevate is not a first-timer program. It targets companies that have already been in business between three and thirty years, owners who cleared the hardest part, staying open, and now need the network and the coaching to grow. Each selected business receives technical assistance, a formal business assessment, featured spotlights that put the company in front of new audiences, and a $5,000 grant on successful completion of the program.

"The grant is not the prize. The prize is being in a building where the right people already know your name before you need them to."

Here is the FOMO that should sit with you. Every one of those 25 owners was a name on an application while somebody else with an equally good business told themselves they would apply next cycle. Cohorts are a closing door by design. There are a fixed number of seats, the review is competitive, and the people who get in are almost always the ones who put their hand up early and told a clear story about where their business is headed. The rest find out they were qualified the day the list drops without them on it.

This is the kind of opportunity ANF pays attention to. Not a coastal mega-round, but a Detroit institution and a Detroit foundation putting real money and real access behind owners who build where they live. In an economy where introductions and relationships decide who gets the next contract, a program that hands you both is worth more than the $5,000 line item suggests.

The details

The Program

Detroit Elevate  ·  TechTown Detroit

A business growth initiative run by TechTown Detroit and supported by the Gilbert Family Foundation. The 2026 cohort of 25 businesses receives technical assistance, a business assessment, featured spotlights, and a $5,000 grant on completion, backed by a $950,000 foundation investment.

Who Can Apply

3 to 30 years in business  ·  Detroit

Established Detroit businesses that have operated between three and thirty years. The 2026 cohort spans construction, technology, professional services, food, retail, beauty and the creative arts, so most established owners across industries fit the profile.

How To Apply

2026 cohort closed  ·  Watch for the next round

The 2026 cohort is already selected. Applications for future rounds open through TechTown Detroit. Visit detroitelevate.org and techtowndetroit.org to review eligibility, join the interest list, and be ready the moment the next application window opens.

If you want the checklist for next time: confirm your business has been operating at least three years, get your basics clean, revenue, headcount, a one paragraph story of where you are trying to go, and register your interest at detroitelevate.org now so the application does not catch you off guard. The owners who make cohorts like this are not the biggest businesses in the room. They are the ones who were ready when the door opened.

"Access compounds. It only goes to the ones who put their hand up, and it always goes to them early."

Twenty-five Detroit businesses are about to get a year of coaching, exposure and a room full of people who can move their next deal. That is the version of opportunity that does not announce itself twice. The next list is already being built. Whether your name is on it is a decision you make now, not when applications open.
