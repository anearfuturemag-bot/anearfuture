The AI Boom Is Booming. Black Founders Still Can't Get In.

New Crunchbase data shows venture dollars for Black founders stayed scarce even as AI funding broke records. The rising tide is not lifting every boat.

A Near Future Editorial

July 2026

There is a boom happening in venture capital right now, and its name is AI. Record rounds, record valuations, money moving faster than it has in years. New Crunchbase data confirms what the celebration left out: almost none of that surge reached Black founders.

That is the tell. When capital floods in and one group is still standing outside, the issue is not the weather. It is the structure. A boom that only reaches the people who were already connected is not really a boom for everyone. It is a wall with better lighting.

"A boom that only reaches the already-connected isn't a boom. It's a wall with better lighting."

The scarcity is not new. What is new is the contrast. It is one thing to be underfunded in a lean year. It is another to watch historic amounts of capital move past you toward the same familiar faces during the biggest funding wave in a decade.

This is why platforms that surface overlooked builders matter more, not less, right now. When the mainstream is looking in one direction, the work of finding and backing the people it is ignoring becomes the whole job. A Near Future exists to point the spotlight somewhere the crowd is not.

The next founder to define a category is already building. The only question is who is paying attention early enough to matter.
