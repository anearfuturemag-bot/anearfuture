There's $100,000 on the Table for Metro Detroit Entrepreneurs

Beasley Media Group and the MEDC just launched a $100,000 small business grant program. No pitch deck required, just a business and a plan.

A Near Future Editorial

July 2026

$100K

Available now for Metro Detroit small businesses

Beasley Media Group Detroit and the Michigan Economic Development Corporation have launched a $100,000 small business grant program specifically for Metro Detroit entrepreneurs. If you run a small business in this region, this is money you need to know about.

The program is built around the idea that access to capital shouldn't require a VC network or a pitch deck. It's designed for the ground-level builder, the person already doing the work, already showing up, already building something real. The grant exists to give that person a financial runway they might not have had otherwise.

"Access to capital shouldn't require a network you were never given access to."

For the entrepreneurs in our community, the ones building without investors, without co-signs, without the resources that larger cities seem to take for granted, this is a concrete opportunity. Not a program you have to wait years to see results from. Not a theoretical investment in infrastructure. Money, now, for businesses that are already moving.

How to apply: Applications are being accepted through Kiss FM Detroit's grant portal. The process is direct: you bring the business, they bring the funding.

Apply Now

Metro Detroit small business owners can apply directly through the program portal. Don't wait on this one.

kissfmdetroit.com/grant →

Opportunities like this don't stay open indefinitely. If you're building something in Metro Detroit, or you know someone who is, share this. The work doesn't stop at building the business. Sometimes the work is making sure the right people hear about the room.
