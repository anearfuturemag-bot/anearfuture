A Detroit Founder Is Paying Students to Start Companies

Ali Kain launched a 1,000 dollar national scholarship, decided by an essay instead of a GPA, for undergraduate students preparing to build their own companies.

A Near Future Editorial

July 2026

$1,000

National scholarship decided by essay, open to any undergraduate

Detroit entrepreneur Ali Kain just launched the Ali Kain Scholarship for Entrepreneurs, a 1,000 dollar national award for undergraduate students who are preparing to build their own companies. It's open to students at any college in the country, with an application deadline of April 15, 2027, and a winner named that May.

What makes it different is how you win it. There's no minimum GPA and no transcript to polish. The award is decided by an essay, where applicants write about their goals, the obstacles they have faced, and what they actually want to build. It rewards the thing a founder needs most, a clear reason why, not a grade point average.

That choice says a lot about who Ali Kain is. He built his own career across technology, logistics, and shipping, coming up by spotting gaps other people missed and working through the obstacles in front of him. Now he's putting his own name and money behind students trying to do the same, from Detroit, for the whole country.

"The best time to back a founder is before anyone else believes in them yet."

The timing is the whole point. Most support for founders shows up late, after there's a product, revenue, or traction, once most of the risk is already gone. This one shows up early, while a student is still balancing classes and a fragile idea and quietly wondering if any of it is real.

A thousand dollars won't build a company. But early money carries a message that outweighs the amount. Someone with a real track record read your story and decided you were worth a bet. For a young founder, that belief can be the difference between quitting and continuing.

How to Apply

  • Award$1,000, granted to one undergraduate student
  • Who can applyAny undergraduate enrolled at a US college, pursuing or planning a career in entrepreneurship. Open nationwide, no city or school restriction.
  • What to submitOne original essay responding to the official prompt, sharing your goals, the challenges you have faced, and your vision for the business you want to build.
  • DeadlineApril 15, 2027
  • Winner announcedMay 15, 2027
  • How to enterSubmissions are accepted through the scholarship’s official channel. Find the full eligibility rules, the essay prompt, and the submission address on the official announcement below.
