Detroit Just Launched a $700,000 Startup Fund

The city's first-of-its-kind fund is designed to fuel job creation and keep talent rooted in Detroit, exactly what the next generation of builders has been waiting for.

A Near Future Editorial

July 2026

$700K

Detroit's first-of-its-kind city startup fund

Detroit just put $700,000 on the table for its builders. The city announced the launch of its first-ever startup fund, a first-of-its-kind initiative designed specifically to fuel job creation, support emerging businesses, and keep Detroit's best talent from leaving for somewhere else.

The fund launched at Newlab inside Michigan Central, a facility that's become one of the clearest symbols of Detroit's current reinvention. The location wasn't incidental. It signals something: this city is investing in innovation infrastructure, and it wants the people it's producing to stay inside it.

"Detroit is building the infrastructure so that its builders don't have to leave to be taken seriously."

For years, one of the most common stories out of Detroit's creative and entrepreneurial community was the one about leaving. About going to New York, Chicago, or Atlanta to find the capital, the networks, and the rooms where decisions get made. This fund doesn't solve all of that, but it chips away at the narrative that Detroit won't bet on its own.

The goal is direct: support startups that create jobs and retain talent. That combination matters. Retention isn't just an economic metric. It's cultural. Every young founder who stays and builds here makes it more possible for the next one to do the same.

For the young entrepreneurs, creatives, and builders ANF covers, the people who haven't made the news yet, who are still in the building phase, this fund represents something beyond the dollars. It represents a city starting to bet on its own future while it's still being written.

That's the work. And it's just beginning.
