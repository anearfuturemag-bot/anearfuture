Detroit Will Pay Founders $2,000 a Month to Build Here

Detroit's Tech Founder Residency Fellowship pays up to 8 startup founders 2,000 dollars a month for a year to live and build in the city.

A Near Future Editorial

July 2026

$2,000

Monthly stipend the fellowship pays a founder to build in Detroit

Detroit decided the best way to keep its young founders is to pay them to stay. The city's Tech Founder Residency Fellowship gives up to eight startup founders a 2,000 dollar monthly stipend for a full year to live in Detroit and build their companies, plus free workspace at TechTown, Bamboo, and Newlab at Michigan Central.

The target is specific and smart. It is built for early-stage, venture-backable founders who graduated from a Michigan college or university after May 2022 and will commit to living in Detroit for twelve straight months. In other words, it is aimed squarely at the young talent that usually gets recruited straight out of school to a coast.

"Cities lose founders the same way they lose graduates. Detroit decided to pay them to stay."

The stipend is the honest hook. Twelve months of covered living expenses removes the exact pressure that pushes a new founder to take a safe job somewhere else instead of building. Pair that with free space inside the city's best startup hubs, and a founder gets a real runway to try before the runway runs out.

It is a talent-retention play as much as a startup play. Every founder who stays and builds here is jobs, energy, and proof that you do not have to leave Detroit to make it. That is the flywheel the city is trying to start.

How It Works

  • Stipend$2,000 per month for one year, plus free membership at TechTown, Bamboo, and Newlab
  • WhoEarly-stage, venture-backable founders who graduated a Michigan college or university after May 2022 and commit to 12 months living in Detroit
  • CohortUp to 8 fellows
  • ApplyingThe inaugural cohort’s applications ran through December 1 via MakeMyMove. Watch the City of Detroit page below for the next round.
See the City of Detroit announcement

The first cohort has been selected. Check the official page for a future application window before applying.

This is the kind of bet ANF wants more cities to make. Keep your builders by backing them where they already are. Detroit paying founders to stay is the opposite of the brain drain everyone accepts as normal.

The talent was always here. Detroit is finally paying to keep it.
