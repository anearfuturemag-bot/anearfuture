A Detroit Startup Raised $11.5 Million to Build AI-Run Metal Factories

Nox Metals closed an 11.5 million dollar seed round led by Hyperion, with Y Combinator and Palmer Luckey, to reindustrialize American manufacturing from Detroit.

A Near Future Editorial

July 2026

$11.5M

Seed round Nox Metals raised to scale its Detroit factory

Nox Metals, a Detroit company building AI-run metal factories, just closed an 11.5 million dollar seed round. It was led by Hyperion, with backing from Y Combinator, Oculus founder Palmer Luckey, Operator Collective, DTX Ventures, and others. Founder and CEO Zane Hengsperger expects the round to grow to 14 million.

The pitch is blunt: reindustrialize America, starting in Detroit. Nox builds vertically integrated factories that run on AI to cut the human bottlenecks out of getting raw material out the door. It supplies custom-cut metals, mostly aluminum plate and bar, to about 100 customers nationwide, from small machine shops to defense and space companies like Anduril and SpaceX.

"A metal shop that thinks like a software company, built in the city that used to build everything."

There is something fitting about this happening here. The city that put the world on wheels is now home to a startup trying to rebuild American manufacturing with modern tools. Hengsperger plans to use the money to grow the team and expand the Detroit headquarters, not to relocate to a coast.

That choice matters. The easy version of this story is a founder taking coastal money and coastal talent somewhere else. Nox is doing the opposite, betting that the hard, physical work of making things still belongs here, and that AI makes Detroit a place to build the future of industry rather than a monument to its past.

This is the kind of builder ANF exists to spotlight. Someone who looks at Detroit's industrial bones and sees a head start, not a ruin. Nox is wiring old-economy muscle to new-economy tools, in the city best equipped to prove it works.

Detroit built the last industrial century. A startup here wants to build the next one.
