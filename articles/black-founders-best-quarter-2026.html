Black Founders Just Had Their Best Quarter Since 2022. There's a Catch.

Funding for Black founders hit its highest quarterly total in years. The bottleneck was never talent. It is access to the room where the decisions get made.

A Near Future Editorial

July 2026

2022

Black founders’ strongest funding quarter since

Black founders just posted their highest quarterly venture total since 2022. That is real, and it is worth naming. But the reporting came with a catch that anyone paying attention already understood: the number went up, and the underlying problem did not move.

The problem was never a shortage of talent. It is access. Warm introductions, closed networks, and the invisible rooms where checks actually get written still decide who gets funded long before a pitch deck is ever opened.

"Talent is everywhere. Access isn't. That gap has a name, and it isn't ability."

A good quarter inside a broken system is still a good quarter, and founders should take the win. But a single strong data point does not fix a pipeline that was built to run through people who already had the connections.

It helps to be precise about what improved and what didn't. The dollars going to Black founders rose. The share of total venture going to Black founders, the number that actually measures access, barely moved. A bigger slice of a much bigger pie can still be a sliver, and a strong quarter can coexist with a system that hasn't changed its habits.

The fix isn't mysterious. It's the boring, structural work of widening who gets seen: more scouts looking outside the usual networks, more capital allocated on purpose, more platforms putting overlooked founders in front of the people who write checks. Attention is upstream of funding, and attention is a choice.

This is the exact gap A Near Future was built to close. Our whole reason for existing is to put a spotlight on builders before the traditional gatekeepers do, to widen the room instead of guarding it. Funding follows attention. If the attention only ever finds the already connected, the funding will too.

Tag a founder building without the room right now. That is where the next great company is hiding.
