A TikTok Soundbite Became a Hot 100 Hit in About a Year

Bella Kay, 20, went from a viral TikTok ballad to a Hot 100 hit and a debut album, signed to Atlantic and named to Billboard's 21 Under 21.

A Near Future Editorial

July 2026

2.6B

TikTok views on the ballad that turned Bella Kay from a soundbite into a star.

A little over a year ago, Bella Kay was a soundbite on TikTok. Now she is a 20-year-old with a debut album and a Hot 100 hit. The Houston-raised, Orlando-based singer-songwriter turned an app moment into an actual music career, fast.

The turning point was a ballad. Her song The Sick went viral, passing 210 million streams and generating more than 2.6 billion views on TikTok, the kind of number most artists never touch. She did not let it be a one-off. Her single iloveitiloveitiloveit climbed to number 17 on the Hot 100 this spring, and she released her debut album, My Reckless Abandon, in July.

The industry noticed. Now signed to Atlantic and named to Billboard's 21 Under 21, Kay went from a viral clip to a global artist in the span of about a year, on the strength of songs people actually connect to rather than a single lucky post.

"A TikTok soundbite became a Hot 100 hit in about a year."

The takeaway is not go viral. Plenty of artists get a viral moment and vanish. Kay had real songs ready and a point of view behind them, so when the attention came, she had somewhere to send it. That is how a moment becomes a career.

This is the modern path ANF keeps pointing at. Build the work first, so when the internet finally looks, you have something real to show it.

The app made the moment. The songs made the career.
