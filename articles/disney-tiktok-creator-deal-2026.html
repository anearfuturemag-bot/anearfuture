Disney Just Handed Its Vault to Creators.

A first-of-its-kind deal lets TikTok creators build with Marvel, Star Wars and Pixar footage, and the best of it lands on Disney+. The gatekept vault just opened. The question is who moves first.

A Near Future Editorial

August 2026

For as long as there has been a Disney, its characters have been the most protected intellectual property on earth. You did not touch Mickey, Marvel, or the Star Wars universe without a legal team and a licensing budget most people never see in a lifetime. This month, that wall came down for the exact people it always kept out.

Disney and TikTok announced a first-of-its-kind global content-sharing deal that lets creators build short-form videos using characters and scenes from hundreds of Disney titles, spanning Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, and FX. It is the first agreement of its kind between the platform and a traditional media company, and it flips a decades-old relationship. For years, using Disney footage was the fastest way to get a video taken down. Now it is an invitation.

Hundreds

of Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars and FX titles opening to creators

The distribution side is where it gets serious. A curated selection of creator videos will not only stream on TikTok, they will appear inside a new vertical video tab on Disney+ built to capture the younger audience that lives in short-form. That is the first time TikTok videos will run on another platform at all. Read that again: a creator with a phone and an idea can now end up on the same service people pay a monthly bill to watch.

"Access was never the wall. The wall was permission, and Disney just took it down."

There is a support layer too. Through a jointly run Disney Creator Ambassador Program, the two companies say they will give standout creators increased visibility, access to exclusive events, and actual career development pathways. In plain terms, this is not just a licensing loophole. It is a talent pipeline, and the door is open to people who were never on a studio's radar.

Here is the ANF read. The biggest IP vault in the world just became raw material for anyone willing to make something with it. The advantage does not go to whoever has the most followers today. It goes to whoever understands these characters, has a real point of view, and starts building while everyone else is still deciding whether this is a big deal. It is a big deal.

This is the pattern young builders should train themselves to see. Every few years a gate that used to require money, permission, or connections quietly swings open, and a small group of people who were already sharpening their craft walk straight through it. The ones who win are almost never the ones who waited for a clearer signal. They are the ones who were making things before the opportunity was obvious.

How to be early: the deal rolls out in the U.S. in the coming months, so the runway to get ready is now. Watch for the Disney Creator Ambassador Program and the Disney and TikTok official channels for the launch and application details, sharpen a lane you actually know inside one of these universes, and have a body of work ready before the tools go live. Early creators define what a new format looks like. Late ones copy it.

Save this if you build. The vault is open, the feed is waiting, and the only thing standing between you and it is whether you make the thing.
